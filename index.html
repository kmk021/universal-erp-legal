Universal ERP is business software for billing, inventory, payments and customer management. Businesses that use it (“our clients”) can connect their own WhatsApp Business account through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) to send invoices, quotations, payment reminders and, where their customers have agreed, offers and greetings.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Universal ERP handles, why, and how it is protected. MANIKANTA ERP (“we”, “us”) provides the software; each client business remains responsible for the data it enters about its own customers.

Information we handle

  • Client account data: user names, roles and login details of the client's staff.
  • Business records entered by the client: customers and suppliers (name, mobile number, email, address, GSTIN), items, invoices, quotations, payments and expenses.
  • WhatsApp connection data: when a client connects WhatsApp through Meta's sign-up flow, we store the client's WhatsApp Business Account ID, phone number ID and an access token issued by Meta, so the software can send messages on the client's behalf.
  • Message records: for each WhatsApp message the software sends: recipient number, message text, attached document name, time, and delivery status reported by Meta (sent, delivered, read, failed), plus opt-out replies such as “STOP”.

How the information is used

  • To run the client's billing, stock and accounts, and to create documents such as invoices and quotations.
  • To send WhatsApp and email messages that the client asks the software to send: documents, payment reminders, and promotional messages only to customers who have not opted out.
  • To show delivery status and keep a message log for the client.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except as described below.

Sharing

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp): message content, recipient numbers and attachments are sent to Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform to be delivered. Meta's handling is described in the WhatsApp Business Policy and WhatsApp Privacy Policy.
  • Service providers that host and run the software: database and file hosting (Supabase), PDF rendering and email delivery providers, each bound to process data only to provide their service.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, regulation or a valid order of an authority in India.

Storage and security

  • Data is stored on secure cloud servers with encryption in transit (HTTPS) and access restricted by role-based permissions.
  • WhatsApp access tokens are kept only on the server side and are never shown in the application or sent to browsers.
  • Shared document links expire automatically (normally after 7 days).

Retention

Business records are kept while the client uses the service and as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires (generally 8 years for GST records). WhatsApp connection data is deleted when the client disconnects WhatsApp or closes the account. Message logs are kept for up to 12 months.

Customer choices

  • A customer who receives promotional WhatsApp messages can reply STOP at any time; the software then stops sending them offers and greetings. Replying START turns them back on.
  • Customers may ask the client business, or us, to see, correct or delete their personal data (see Data Deletion).

Children

Universal ERP is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes are announced to clients in the application.

Terms of Service

  • Use of WhatsApp: clients must follow the WhatsApp Business Policy and Commerce Policy, send promotional messages only to customers who have agreed to receive them, and honour opt-outs.
  • Message charges: Meta charges per message according to its published rates. These charges are billed by Meta to the payment method the client adds to its own WhatsApp Business account.
  • Client data: the client owns the data it enters and is responsible for having a lawful basis to store and message its customers.
  • Acceptable use: the software must not be used for spam, fraud, harassment, or any unlawful purpose. We may suspend messaging for accounts that misuse it.
  • Availability: we work to keep the service available but do not guarantee uninterrupted delivery; WhatsApp delivery also depends on Meta.
  • Liability: to the extent permitted by law, our liability is limited to the fees paid for the service in the previous three months.
  • Governing law: these terms are governed by the laws of India; courts at Jangaon, Telangana have jurisdiction.

Data Deletion

To delete your data:

  • Client businesses: an admin can disconnect WhatsApp in Settings → WhatsApp Sending, which deletes the stored WhatsApp connection data immediately. To close the account and delete all business records, email us from the account owner's address.
  • Customers of a client business: email us with your mobile number and the name of the business that messaged you. We will remove your personal data (subject to records the business must keep by law) and confirm within 30 days.
  • People who connected through Facebook / Meta: you can also remove the Universal ERP app from your Facebook settings (Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations); email us to delete any data already stored.
